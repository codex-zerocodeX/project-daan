## Official global rule

Source: [OpenAI Build Week](https://openai.com/build-week/) and [Devpost rule](https://openai.devpost.com/rules).

- Category: Apps for Your Life, Work and Productivity, Developer Tools, or Education.
- Rubric: technological implementation, design, potential impact, and quality of the idea, equally weighted.
- Tie-break begins with technological implementation.
- Global work may be new during July 13–21 or a meaningful extension created during that period.
- Submission requires a working/testable project, description, public YouTube demo under three minutes, repository, Codex collaboration explanation, and main `/feedback` task ID.
- Reused data must be authorized or licensed.
- A registered entrant whose free credits are exhausted may request USD 100 in build credit by July 17 at 12:00 PM PT, while supplies last and subject to approval; the credit must be used by July 31.

## Local rule confirmed (official microsite, July 18)

- All project work starts Saturday: no pre-built project; idea and team may form beforehand, but code and prototype must begin at the event.
- Judging criterion: working build quality, usefulness, and visible Codex impact.
- Core functionality must demonstrate working state, not future promise.
- Submission: team lead submits project, working link, team detail, problem statement, and evidence of Codex contribution.
- Five finalists give a live demo (3:45–4:15 PM block, so roughly 5–6 minutes per team including transition).
- Solo attendance permitted; organizer facilitates team matching.
- Never forward or publicly share a credit claim link; confirm receipt at the help desk.
