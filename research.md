## Historical answer at the July 14 cutoff

Do not lock Tumpak yet. Run a bounded live decision between three materially different products:

1. **Saan Tayo?** — a transparent Philippine program-site comparison surface over a rights-cleared Maphy indicator subset;
2. **Tumpak** — an authoritative change-to-test gate whose prior-art claim is now narrower;
3. **CleanRoute** — a fully local proof that an AI request route was tampered with, quarantined, and rerouted.

The current expected-value lead is **Saan Tayo?**, not because it is universally novel, but because it combines the strongest visual design, social-impact story, and a difficult-to-reproduce Philippine data advantage. Tumpak remains tied on the internal rubric and has the cleanest technical proof. CleanRoute remains only if a harmless local fixture works; otherwise its security and integration risk are too high.

No strategy is foolproof. The decision rule is empirical: the first candidate that passes rights, complete state change, three-run reliability, one-user recognition, 90-second comprehension, and honest-comparable gates wins. The full gates are in [`idea.md`](idea.md).

## Earlier focused Tumpak thesis — retained as finalist research

Tumpak detects a changed official fact, turns the semantic change into an executable regression case, catches an agent answering from the retired fact, and produces a dated red-to-green proof after repair.

The prepared live case can use the Philippine Statistics Authority's July 13 PSGC update. The municipality of San Isidro in Davao del Norte became the Municipality of Sawata, while Barangay Sawata became Barangay Poblacion. That double rename creates a memorable entity-lifecycle failure: an answer can be fluent, cited, and historically true while being operationally wrong today.

Its causal story remains:

```text
official source changes
        ↓
GPT-5.6 extracts the entity-level change
        ↓
Tumpak generates an executable case
        ↓
old agent answer fails deterministically
        ↓
bounded update
        ↓
the identical question passes with a dated receipt
```

It is not foolproof and it does not guarantee a prize. The earlier claim that it was the single highest expected-value concept is superseded by the expanded portfolio audit.

## Why this problem is real now

The PSA published its Second Quarter 2026 PSGC update on July 13, one day before this research cutoff. The release contains two official renames and 15 corrected geographic names and links the updated datafile and summary of change. The PSA page also states that site content is public domain unless otherwise stated.

Recent research independently validates the broader failure:

- **HoH** creates temporal QA data from version differences and shows that outdated material can reduce RAG accuracy and mislead the model even when current information is present.
- **FRESCO** finds that retrieval systems can favor older, semantically rich evidence over newer facts.
- **When Facts Change** finds unstable model behavior when retrieved context conflicts with remembered temporal fact.

These papers prove the problem, not Tumpak's market. They also stop us from claiming that freshness evaluation itself is novel.

## Tumpak candidate thesis — formerly selected

### Name and line

**Tumpak**

**When the official fact changes, prove your AI changed with it.**

### User

A developer or product team operating a Philippine student-service, public-service, logistics, research, or support agent over official information that changes.

The first adopter story should remain narrow: a team with a RAG or agent workflow that has no automatic answer-level regression when its authoritative source changes.

### User job

> When an authoritative source changes, tell me which answer is now dangerous, give me a reproducible failing case, and prove the same question is correct after I update the agent.

### Prepared case

Input:

- prior PSGC state;
- PSA Second Quarter 2026 change statement;
- an intentionally stale agent or index.

Question:

> As of June 30, 2026, is Sawata the municipality or the barangay in Davao del Norte, and what is the barangay's current official name?

Expected behavior:

- Municipality: Sawata;
- Barangay: Poblacion;
- old names preserved as historical aliases, not current answer;
- answer bound to the July 13 PSA release and effective date;
- uncertainty or abstention if the source does not establish a requested field.

### Model-native work

GPT-5.6 should:

1. extract semantic change from old/new official text into a strict schema;
2. identify entity lifecycle: rename, merge, retirement, correction, or conflict;
3. generate one or more impacted user questions and expected constraints;
4. propose a bounded repair note after the deterministic failure.

Code—not the model—should verify:

- current entity identifier or canonical name;
- retired alias rejected as current truth;
- required source and effective date present;
- identical case identifier before and after repair;
- red-to-green state transition.

This separation prevents a model from giving itself a passing grade.

### Smallest complete build

One prepared official change, one generated case, one stale answer, one deterministic failure, one bounded update, one identical rerun, and one shareable receipt.

If extra time remains, add two more change types from the same PSA release. Do not build arbitrary connector, authentication, billing, collaboration, a general benchmark platform, or automatic production deployment.

### Screen hierarchy

1. **Changed source** — before, after, authority, effective date.
2. **Affected answer** — the exact user question and stale output.
3. **Why it failed** — entity state and evidence mismatch in plain language.
4. **Repair** — bounded change, not a magical “fix everything” button.
5. **Proof** — identical case, green assertion, timestamp, source link.

### Demo atom

> Yesterday the Philippine map changed. Our stale agent still calls Sawata a barangay under San Isidro. Tumpak reads the official change, generates the question that should now fail, catches the retired fact, and proves the identical question passes after the update.

Build Tumpak only if it wins the reopened decision and all of these are true by Friday night:

## Strategy for Saturday psychology

- Lead with a changed fact, not an architecture diagram.
- Create contrast in the first ten seconds: “correct yesterday, wrong today.”
- Show one failure at human scale before showing a score.
- Let the red receipt create tension; let the identical green rerun resolve it.
- State the honest limit before a judge asks: one public case proves the mechanism, not universal safety.
- Make the judge's retell effortless: “They turn an official update into a test that catches stale AI answers.”
- Keep the source and effective date visible so the product feels operational, not magical.

## Reusable idea-generator rule

For a future event, generate concepts from five variables:

```text
costly failure × authoritative change × unfair mechanism × visible proof × sprint fit
```

Then apply this order:

1. **Rule gate** — eligible track, required technology, data right, new-work boundary.
2. **Pain gate** — named user, observable cost, current workaround.
3. **Proof gate** — one before/after state a judge understands without narration.
4. **Mechanism gate** — model performs a job that ordinary code alone does poorly.
5. **Completion gate** — core can be green in half the available sprint.
6. **Adjacency gate** — find the closest three products and one research benchmark.
7. **Retell gate** — a judge can repeat the idea in one sentence after ten entries.

An idea that fails one gate is rejected or held. A mechanism that strengthens the selected causal loop is merged rather than promoted as another product.

## Commuter cost, fare inflation, toll, and speed synthesis — July 18

The current evidence does not support one national “fare per kilometer” or one “standard km/h” for public transport. Each measure needs a mode and authority boundary:

- LTFRB's effective matrices still put traditional PUJ at PHP 13 for the first 4 km plus PHP 1.80/succeeding km; modern PUJ at PHP 15 for 4 km plus PHP 1.80 non-air-conditioned or PHP 2.20 air-conditioned; city bus at PHP 13 ordinary or PHP 15 air-conditioned for the first 5 km plus its class add-on; and UV Express at PHP 2.40/km traditional or PHP 2.50/km modern.
- The broad March 17, 2026 PUV fare increases were suspended on March 18. They are historical approved-but-not-current rates, not the application default.
- Regular taxi combines a PHP 50 flagdown with PHP 13.50/km and PHP 2/minute. TNVS combines class flagdown, distance, time, and potentially dynamic pricing. An app quote is therefore an observed quote, not a fixed national matrix result.
- Tricycle fare is locally governed. Davao's June 2026 advisory uses PHP 10 for the first 4 km plus PHP 1/km; Quezon City's documented ordinance uses PHP 9/person regular, PHP 18 special trip, and PHP 1/succeeding km. Neither can be projected to another LGU.
- LRT-2 and MRT-3 have had an across-the-board 50% discount since March 23, 2026. LRT-1 is not included in that universal program. NCR PNR commuter operation remains suspended.
- June 2026 national transport inflation was 12.8% year on year versus 6.4% all-item inflation; the national transport index rose 29.81% from June 2021 to June 2026. CPI is broader than fare and must not be applied as a direct fare multiplier.
- Actual regulated component changes differ: the Oct. 2023 traditional-PUJ base rose 8.33%, the Mar. 2024 regular-taxi flagdown rose 11.11%, and the Jul. 2026 SLEX Alabang–Calamba Class 1 toll rose 8.73%. Each applies only to the named component and effective adjustment.
- RA 4136 default maximum speed for bus/truck is 50 km/h on qualifying open-country road, 30 km/h on through or ordinary city street, and 20 km/h in crowded/dangerous conditions. A legal maximum is not an observed commute speed or an arrival-time estimate.
- Toll is a gate-pair, expressway, and vehicle-class cost to the vehicle/operator. It may affect a commuter's route quote, but it is not a universal regulated per-passenger surcharge.

The complete matrix, calculation, change timeline, evidence boundary, and gap register are in [`transport-cost.md`](transport-cost.md). This research adds a future cost-receipt option; it does not change the current walkability score or pretend the fixture-backed application has live fare data.
