Good to know

- Bring a valid photo ID — guards check at every gate.
- Driving without an Ateneo sticker? Gate 3 is your only entry. You'll leave your ID at the claiming post (behind Areté) and exit through the same area to get it back.
- Guest parking is near Areté (Northwest Car Park). Ask the guard or event team for the current parking instructions when you arrive.
- Walking? Best entry is Gate 3 — the gate connected to Regis. Gate 3.5 works too (it's the only gate open on Sundays).
- Commuting? Jeepneys & buses along Katipunan — get off at the Gate 3.5 stop. LRT-2 Katipunan Station is a short jeep / tricycle hop away.
- Free campus e-jeep (Line A): JSEC Mall → Old Rizal Library — alight there, 3 min on foot to Leong Hall.
- Heading home by car: exit through the same gate area you entered (that's where your ID is) — Gate 3.5 northbound, Gate 3.2 southbound.
