04 / Before you go

The useful
small print.

Everything worth knowing before you enter Leong Hall.

Can I bring something I already built?+

No. Ideas and teams can exist beforehand, but all code, prototypes, and design work for the local Buildathon must begin at the event.

Do I need a team?+

No. You can arrive solo. We will help people looking for teammates meet one another before the first build block.

What should I bring?+

Bring a laptop, charger, and the accounts or tools you normally use to build. A laptop is strongly recommended for the room setup.

How do submissions work?+

One person submits for each team. The submission asks for your project, team, working links, the problem you are solving, and evidence of how Codex helped. Final submissions close at 3:00 PM.

How do I get event credits?+

Check in using the same email you registered with. A volunteer will send your individual API and Codex credit links to that address. Do not forward those links.

Can I walk in without a confirmed registration?+

No. Capacity, food, and credit allocation are based on the confirmed attendee list.
