## What we are not going to do

- Build a generic startup idea validator. The market is crowded and the local corpus alone does not create enough differentiation.
- Build a general research-commercialization chatbot. JuanaKNOW is a direct Philippine adjacent product.
- Build a map, knowledge graph, marketplace, and agent in one day. Those are multiple products, not one demo.
- Claim general accuracy from one to three prepared cases.
- Claim patentability, freedom to operate, legal advice, or universal novelty.
- Publish private `/blead` data, personal contact detail, or credential.
- Add auth, billing, collaboration, arbitrary provider support, arbitrary connector, or custom case authoring before judging.
- Infer the local panel from the global judge list.
- Optimize for a biography instead of the published rubric.
